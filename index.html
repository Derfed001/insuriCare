Getting a quote

Documents we usually need

  • Everyone: copy of national ID or passport, KRA PIN, phone number and email.
  • Car: logbook or registration copy, make, model, year and estimated value.
  • Medical and life: date of birth of each person covered, and birth certificates for children.
  • Business or group: company KRA PIN, registration certificate, staff list with dates of birth and roles, and annual payroll for WIBA.

This is a typical list. Your insurer may ask for more depending on the cover.

When something happens

If you need to claim

  1. Make sure everyone is safe and get medical help if needed.
  2. Call or WhatsApp us on +254 115 129 088 as soon as you can, ideally within 24 hours.
  3. Have your policy number ready.
  4. Take photos. Report theft or road accidents to the police and get the abstract or OB number.
  5. Do not repair, dispose of property or admit liability before your insurer approves.
  6. Keep all receipts and send the documents we ask for.
Renewing or switching

Before you renew

  • Check the sum insured still matches the real value of what you are covering.
  • Read the exclusions and the excess you would pay on a claim.
  • Compare the same level of cover across insurers, not just the price.
  • Note your renewal date so cover never lapses.
  • Tell us about changes: a new car, a new address, more dependants or more staff.

Ask us to compare options. We handle the paperwork if you switch.
